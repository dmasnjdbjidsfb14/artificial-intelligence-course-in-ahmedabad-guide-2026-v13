Understanding the Essentials of AI

Before embarking on your journey with an [Artificial Intelligence Course in Ahmedabad](https://globalitians.com/CoursesDetails/artificial-intelligence-course-in-ahmedabad), it's essential to grasp the foundational concepts of AI. The field encompasses a wide array of sub-disciplines, including machine learning, natural language processing, robotics, and computer vision.


  Machine Learning (ML): The study of algorithms that enable computers to learn from data.
  Natural Language Processing (NLP): The ability of machines to understand and respond to human language.
  Computer Vision: Teaching machines to interpret and make decisions based on visual data.
  Robotics: The design and creation of intelligent agents capable of performing tasks autonomously.
  Deep Learning: A subset of machine learning that uses neural networks for high-level abstractions.
  Reinforcement Learning: A type of learning where an agent learns to make decisions through trial and error.
